Summary:
Protect trips whose latest OperationalQueue status is WAITING_DISPATCH or PENDING_DISPATCH from being auto-marked NO_SHOW by recovery/force-complete flows.

Files changed:
- apps/api/src/modules/operational-flow/operational-flow.service.ts (recoverStaleTrips, forceCompleteRoute)

What changed:
- recoverStaleTrips: skip marking trip NO_SHOW when latest queue status is WAITING_DISPATCH or PENDING_DISPATCH (added log).
- forceCompleteRoute: skip forcing NO_SHOW for trips whose latest queue status is WAITING_DISPATCH or PENDING_DISPATCH (added log).

Root cause:
Recovery jobs used route.date and boardedAt to decide staleness and forced NO_SHOW without checking queue dispatch status. Queues still PENDING_DISPATCH / WAITING_DISPATCH must not be auto-finalized.

Validation steps (after deploy):
1) SQL (Postgres):
   SELECT id, "patientId", "appointmentDate", status, "noShowAt"
   FROM "OperationalQueue"
   WHERE status = 'NO_SHOW' AND "noShowAt" >= now() - interval '24 hours' AND "appointmentDate" > "noShowAt"
   ORDER BY "noShowAt" DESC;
2) Watch logs for:
   - "[RECOVERY] [OPS] skipping auto-NO_SHOW for tripId="
   - "[FINALIZE] skipping tripId="
